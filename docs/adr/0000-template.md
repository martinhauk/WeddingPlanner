# ADR NNNN: Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR NNNN | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who decided (roles, not personal names)

## Context

What problem or force makes a decision necessary? Constraints, requirements, facts verified (link
sources), and what is still unknown.

## Decision

What we do, stated plainly ("We use …"). Include the concrete rules other contributors must follow.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder. Costs, risks and follow-up work. How we would notice that the
decision was wrong.

## Alternatives considered

- **Alternative A:** why not.
- **Alternative B:** why not.
